Your closest friends, quietly by your side.

Up to 12 close friends become tiny pixel animals on your screen. See presence, typing, and short messages without interrupting what you are doing.

●

Presence you can see

Friends stroll when online, doze when away, and curl up to sleep when offline, with a matching status dot.

•••

Typing and short messages

Typing dots appear only while someone uses the SIDEY composer. Sent messages briefly sit above their character and are deleted from the server after three days.

×7

Double-click for a bigger hello

Double-click your character to make it grow for a moment and catch your friends' attention. Your message bubble is easier to spot too.
