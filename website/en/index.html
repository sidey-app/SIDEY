Private ambient messenger · Alpha

Your closest friends, quietly by your side.

Up to five close friends become tiny pixel animals on your screen. See presence, typing, and short messages without interrupting what you are doing.

↓ Download for macOS

macOS 26+ · Apple Silicon · Alpha Windows 11 25H2 x64 · In development

Close connection

Just enough connection, without opening another chat window.

See that your friends are around, then share a quick thought when it matters.

●

Presence you can see

Friends stroll when online, doze when away, and curl up to sleep when offline, with a matching status dot.

•••

Typing and short messages

Typing dots appear only while someone uses the SIDEY composer. Sent messages briefly sit above their character.

↘

Click-through by default

The overlay passes pointer input to the app behind it. Only your character and the composer are explicitly interactive.

Privacy boundary

Your friends' presence, not what is on your screen.

The only global activity signals are time since the last system input and screen-lock state. Typing status comes only from the SIDEY composer.

  • Screen contents
  • Active app lists
  • Keys typed in other apps
  • Mouse coordinates
  • File contents
  • Microphone or camera data
!

A note about the Alpha

E2EE is not currently provided. SIDEY also does not guarantee display above secure screens, DRM apps, elevated apps, or every exclusive-fullscreen game.
