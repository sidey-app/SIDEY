## About

SIDEY is a desktop messenger for macOS and Windows that lets you chat with friends through 2D pixel animals along the edge of your screen. Each character represents a real friend, showing their status and short messages while you work or relax.

![SIDEY on macOS — pixel animal friends exchange messages along the bottom of the screen.](../assets/sidey-overlay-preview.png)

- **Keep doing your thing:** Click and use the apps behind SIDEY as usual while it stays on screen.
- **See how your friends are doing:** Walking, dozing, and sleeping animations let you see whether your friends are online at a glance.
- **Say a little something:** Open SIDEY to send a short message or share a playful moment.

### macOS

**Requirements:** macOS 26 or later · Apple Silicon. Intel Macs aren’t supported.

<details>
  <summary><strong>Download a .dmg from GitHub</strong></summary>
  <br/>

</details>

Contributions are welcome, whether you’re fixing bugs, improving docs, translating, or creating characters.
