## About

SIDEY is a desktop messenger for macOS and Windows that lets you chat with friends through 2D pixel animals along the edge of your screen. Each character represents a real friend, showing their status and short messages while you work or relax.

![SIDEY on macOS — pixel animal friends exchange messages along the bottom of the screen.](sidey-overlay-preview.png)

- **Keep doing your thing:** Click and use the apps behind SIDEY as usual while it stays on screen.
- **See how your friends are doing:** Walking, dozing, and sleeping animations let you see whether your friends are online at a glance.
- **Say a little something:** Open SIDEY to send a short message or share a playful moment.

## Installation

### macOS

**Requirements:** macOS 26 or later · Apple Silicon. Intel Macs aren’t supported.

<details>
  <summary><strong>Download from the Mac App Store</strong></summary>
  <br/>
  <p>You can easily install SIDEY from the Mac App Store:</p>
  <p>
    <a href="https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/sidey/id6808528060">
      <img src="mac-app-store.en.svg" height="48" alt="Download SIDEY from the Mac App Store">
    </a>
  </p>
  <p>Updates may take longer to arrive because they go through Apple’s review process.</p>
</details>

### Windows

**Requirements:** Windows 10 version 1809 or later · x64.

<details>
  <summary><strong>Download an .exe from GitHub</strong></summary>
  <br/>

  Go to [SIDEY’s GitHub releases](https://github.com/sidey-app/SIDEY/releases), scroll down to **Assets**, and download the installer that matches your system.

</details>
